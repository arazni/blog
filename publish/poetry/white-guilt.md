---
title: White Guilt (2026)
date: 2026-02-01
description: A poem about white supremacist institutions and murders
tags: [poetry]
---

A guilty verdict, a curse, a condemnation, not an internalized shame.

```text
i do not need to ask
  what we said
    about Alex Pretti
i still remember
  what we sang
    about Philando Castile

the apologia album
  plays platinum
its sound stutters
  but it skips no one

a timeless bloodlust chant
  sang before Trayvon
  before Emmett

a discordant trill taught
  to white children in homes
    who are slapped by their conductors
      if caught practicing in public

a battle hymn enforced
  when their parents scream
    at barely rebellious teens

a dead man--
  a lotto ball plucked!
a murdered child--
  a state lawsuit jackpot!

nuke the sand statics!
  my arabic girlfriend
    out of earshot
shame on the scratch lovers!
  my black partner
    a year away

shoot the pregnant stuck
  before she drops anchors!
my latine love and i
  infertile in our home

the only psalms
  i picked up
    donations
    protests
    deliveries
hardly balm me
  let alone anyone else
```

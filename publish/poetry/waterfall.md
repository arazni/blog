# Waterfall (2026)

A lot of bad stuff has happened lately.

```text
may i quit tech
  and go chase a Woolf downstream?

these are the final years
  to fill my pockets with disks
    the compact already infinitely heavier
      than wireless ubiquity
    yet hardly as useful today
      as a good rock

this circuit recycles:
  decades of lives' work
    insert into nothing
    affix onto nothing
    reduce to refuse

the time for reusing disks
  into opalescent mirror tiles
    that drunken reflections
      over banging headboards
is as dead as saying
  dead as disco

when my toes dip into portent
  will i still be scrolling?
when current subsumes me into horizon
  will your feed break the news
    before a friend?
```
